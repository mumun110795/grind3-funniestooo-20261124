## About this entry

The line plays the out-of-office form completely straight — a polite return
date, a courteous note for urgent matters — and bends exactly one element:
the return date. "The 12th" sounds like a day of the month right up until the
second half of the sentence quietly clarifies that it is a month, not a day,
which turns a short break into most of a year without the tone ever changing.
The joke lives entirely in that gap between what the reader assumes and what
the sentence actually promised.
